How to use this plan
  1. 2x compressed pace. Every 90 days of the original plan now takes 45. Target dates and speed bonuses use the compressed dates.
  2. Build first. Start each milestone in the Build column. Open a Learn item the moment you get stuck, not before.
  3. About 70% building, 30% learning. Milestones overlap more now; run two at once.
  4. Speed tactic: watch at 1.5–2x and skip parts you already know. Stop a course the moment you can do the build.
  5. Speed tactic: Claude Code drafts, you verify. Let it write the first version; your job is to understand it, test it and fix it.
  6. Speed tactic: chapters, not books. Read only the chapters listed. Time-box being stuck to 45 minutes, then ask for help.
  7. What doesn't compress: the 2-week journal run, the 30-day demo run, the 3-year audited record. That's market time, not learning time.
  8. Claude mastery is woven in. Items with a gold badge are Claude-specific, and the top card tracks your Claude level. Claude ships features fast: this list is current as of October 2026, and the weekly Anthropic news habit (milestone 1) keeps it fresh.
  9. One path: fund, agents, agency. Every agent you build is part of your trading system first and your agency portfolio second. Learn once, use it twice.
  10. The agency milestones (12 and 14) add real hours, about 8–10 a week from February, once your trading system runs on demo.
  11. Your edge first, ML later. Milestone 3 (writing your rules down precisely) is the flagship. Everything else serves it.
  12. Assume your backtest is lying until it survives walk-forward testing and double the costs.
  13. The Executor is plain code, never an AI. AI can watch, log and explain; it never places orders.
  14. No real money until 30 days on demo with zero manual fixes.
  15. Use Google Colab for heavy work. Your 8 GB laptop will struggle with ML.
  16. Learning tab: Python and maths, day by day. Each study block starts with 30 minutes of maths, then one Python session where you read a real piece of your backtester alongside Grus. Log one line after each session. It's kept separate from the milestones (they measure what you've built, it measures what you understand), and each milestone shows what to learn alongside it.
  17. Expertise tab: deeper study at a lighter pace. It never blocks the build; it's the first thing to drop in a heavy week.
  18. Rest is risk management. A compressed plan is a sprint, and burnout is the biggest threat to it. With a 3:00 AM start, lights out by 9:00 PM on work nights is a hard wall. Your daily schedule is in the card at the top, and it matches your Google Calendar.
